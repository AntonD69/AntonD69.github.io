🌳🏃‍♂️‍➡️ My Parkrun-Fan Keyrings 🔑🏷️

I love making keyrings, and I love Parkruns! The idea for this project came to life at the Homestead Parkrun, where I met a runner who had just completed his 400th event. As I congratulated him, I thought about how special it would be to hand him a custom milestone souvenir to mark the achievement right then and there.

That moment inspired me to start crafting commemorative Parkrun-Fan keyrings to hand out to fellow runners reaching major milestones.

Please note: This project is a personal passion build by a dedicated fan celebrating fellow runners. It is in no way affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Parkrun.

Milestones Available

With design assistance from Silvia and assembly help from Rein, we have designed, laser-cut, and glued keyrings for the following milestones:

  • Official Milestones: 25, 50, 100, 250, 500
  • In-Between Milestones: 150, 200, 300, 350, 400, 450, 600, 700, 800, 900, 1000

Get Yours or Request a Custom Design

If you would like a keyring to celebrate your own achievement or to gift to a running buddy, please reach out via email at keyrings@backToTheAdventure.com!
